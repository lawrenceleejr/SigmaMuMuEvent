A Particle Physics Alumni Reunion
Cocktail hour × Research fair
 
Bring five physicists back.
On 13 December, the US Muon Collider Collaboration opens the first evening of its annual meeting to the people who left.
Not a colloquium. Drinks, then a research fair, at Stanford — for physicists who took their training to industry, and for the colleagues they brought with them.
Here is the thing about that guest list: it is not a mailing list. It is your address book. Every one of us knows five people who used to do this and still miss it — the postdoc who went to a trading desk, the instrumentation wizard now building chips, the friend whose thesis you still cite.
Think of five. Forward this to them. That is the whole ask. Five each and the room fills itself.
And the evening has a headliner. None other than Michael Peskin will give remarks on why reaching the 10 TeV interaction scale is a frontier we have to explore. Over a drink, not from a lectern.
