You're invited
Old friends, new physics.
On the evening of December 13, 2026, the US Muon Collider Collaboration opens its annual meeting at Stanford with a reunion — for the colleagues we miss from the good old days, and for the people they work with now.
Come for the cocktail hour, with a drink in your hand and a room that speaks your language. Stay for the research fair, where our community lays out what a 10 TeV muon collider takes to build and where the interesting problems are. Arguments welcome.
The evening has a headliner. None other than Michael Peskin will give remarks on why reaching the 10 TeV interaction scale is a crucial frontier for us to explore — delivered with a drink in hand.

2 Pass this along to five HEP alumni friends. A note from you carries further than anything we can post — the postdoc who went to a trading desk, the instrumentation wizard now building chips, the friend whose thesis you still cite.
