A PARTICLE PHYSICS ALUMNI REUNION
Cocktail hour × Research fair

On December 13, 2026, the US Muon Collider Collaboration will host an HEP
Alumni Reunion event at Stanford University. The event will kick off this
year's USMCC annual meeting with the colleagues we miss from past lives.

Dust off that particle physics instinct, mix in your new expertise, and enjoy
an evening with friends and colleagues. At the research fair, our community
will lay out what a 10 TeV muon collider takes to build and where the
interesting problems are.

Prof. Michael Peskin (SLAC) will deliver opening remarks on why reaching the
10 TeV interaction scale is a crucial frontier for us to explore.

SUNDAY, DECEMBER 13, 2026
4:30 – 6:30 p.m. Stanford University
